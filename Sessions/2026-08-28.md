## Decisions

- **Feedback from the panel goes into `FEEDBACK.md`**, not `LEARNINGS.md`. The old rule
  ("append to LEARNINGS.md") could not work: the panel had read-only access to this checkout, so
  every entry cost a prompt, and raw friction notes would dilute a curated document. A
  `SessionStart` hook counts the open entries; `/feedback-triage` empties them.
- **The panel half lives in `~/.claude`, not in the repo** — the rule keyed on `$FIGMACLAUDE`
  and one narrow `Edit(…/FEEDBACK.md)` permission, both global so it works from any project.
  `bin/fig-feedback-setup` installs that half on a second machine; `.gitattributes` gives
  `FEEDBACK.md` `merge=union` so two machines appending do not conflict.
- **FigmaClaude gets a native host**, `swift-host/` on `feat/swift-host`. Go was the starting
  idea and was dropped: it has no native macOS UI, and the terminal emulator is the whole cost.
  Swift plus SwiftTerm measured ~10 MB/s throughput against Claude Code's 0.1–3 KB bursts,
  no measurable idle CPU, 2 MB bundle against 242 MB, 46 MB RSS against ~400 MB over five
  processes.
- **The Electron host stays until the Swift one is proven.** Both share
  `~/.figma-ds-cli/panel.json` and `panel-window.json`, so switching keeps the working directory
  and the window.
- **The status line producer is the app itself** (`FigmaClaude --statusline`). That removes the
  Node dependency the migration plan had listed as its one stumbling block.
- **Layout claims are measured, not eyeballed.** `--render-chrome`, `--render-statusline`,
  `--hover` and `measureRow()` exist because `screencapture` is blind here without Screen
  Recording permission.
